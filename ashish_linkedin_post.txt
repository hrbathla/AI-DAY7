🧠 I used to use ONE Claude model for everything.

That was my biggest mistake.

After weeks of trial, error, and learning — here's the model strategy I now follow every single day as part of my ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge 👇

---

⚡ HAIKU → Speed tasks
Hashtags, captions, quick definitions, grammar checks.
When speed > depth.

⚖️ SONNET → My daily workhorse (80% of work)
LinkedIn posts, HR analytics learning, business strategy, research summaries, agentic AI exploration.
Smart + fast = my default.

🔴 OPUS → High-stakes deep work
30-day roadmaps, leadership reports, complex architecture design.
Only when it truly matters.

---

And it's not just about the model.

It's also about EFFORT LEVEL:

→ Low effort: throwaway tasks
→ Standard effort: my daily default ✅
→ High effort: complex structured outputs
→ Max effort: capstone work for leadership

---

Here's the rule I live by now:

"The model matters less than the prompt."

Sonnet 4.6 with a strong role-based prompt beats Opus with a vague one — every time.

I teach prompting. Now I apply it to myself. 🎯
